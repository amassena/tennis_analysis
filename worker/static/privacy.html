Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-27

What this app does

Tennis Uploader records or picks a video on your iPhone and uploads it to a private server (operated by the app's developer) for automated analysis. After processing, the video is viewable in a gallery shared among invited users.

What we collect

  • Your email address: either supplied directly (web sign-in via emailed magic link) or relayed by Apple when you sign in with Apple. Your account identifier on our side is a one-way hash of this email so the same email always resolves to the same private gallery regardless of which sign-in method you use.
  • Your Apple subject identifier (if you signed in with Apple) — a stable per-app identifier. We use it only to recognize you across sessions; it isn't shared or sold.
  • Videos you choose to upload: the original file you picked or recorded, plus a timestamp and a filename. Videos are stored in our cloud storage and processed for tennis-shot detection and coaching feedback.
  • Upload metadata: the byte size, upload time, and processing status — needed to show progress and surface failures.
  • Share links you create: when you tap "Share" on a video, we store an opaque token that lets viewers of that link play that single video without signing in. The mapping is removed when you delete your account.

What we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contact list, calendar, or other Photos library beyond the videos you explicitly pick.
  • No third-party analytics, ad SDKs, or tracking pixels.
  • No microphone audio outside of the video clip you choose to record.

Who sees your videos

Each account has a private gallery at a per-user URL (/u/<hash>). Only you can view it while signed in; other invited users cannot see your videos and you cannot see theirs.

If you generate a share link for a specific video, anyone with that link can view that one video (without signing in) until you revoke it by deleting the link or the video. Don't share the link in public channels if you want it kept private.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes the user record on our server and deletes every video you uploaded (source file, processed outputs, thumbnails, and coaching analysis). Deletion is immediate; the cached gallery page is refreshed within ~24 hours.

Data retention

Videos and their processing outputs are retained indefinitely while your account is active. They are removed on account deletion. Inactive accounts (no sign-in for 12 months) may have their data purged at the operator's discretion.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is over HTTPS. Server- side files live in Cloudflare R2, scoped by your per-user prefix so other users can't read them. Sessions use a short-lived JWT cookie set after sign-in, and any 401/403 response from the server bypasses CDN caches so an unauthenticated probe can't poison a signed-in session. Magic-link emails are throttled and consumed once. We don't share your data with third parties beyond Resend (the email-delivery service that sends sign-in links).

Children

This app is not intended for children under 13.
