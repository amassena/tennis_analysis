Tennis Uploader — Support

Last updated: 2026-05-22

What this app does

Tennis Uploader records or imports tennis videos from your iPhone and uploads them to a private cloud gallery for automated analysis. It's a personal tool maintained for a small group of friends.

Need help?

Email amassena@gmail.com. Typical response is within 1-3 business days.

When you email, please include:

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead (and any error message you saw)
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version

Common questions

Sign-in is by invitation only. If you receive a "Not approved" message after signing in with Apple, contact the email above to be added to the invite list.

Where are my videos? Tap the Gallery tab in the app, or visit tennis.playfullife.com.

How do I delete my account and uploads? Settings (gear icon, top-left of Upload tab) → "Delete my account". This removes your user record and all videos you've uploaded.

WiFi only? By default the app uploads on WiFi only to save your cellular data. Toggle off in Settings if you want cellular uploads.
